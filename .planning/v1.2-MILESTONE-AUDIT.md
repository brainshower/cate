### Integration Warning

`REQ-014` requires only `hostEligible: true` and `status: current` tools to be registered. Current `registry.ts` eligibility returns true unless a status is one of `deprecated`, `unavailable`, or `removed`, or `hostEligible` is explicitly false. That means missing `hostEligible` or missing/non-current status can pass. This contradicts the roadmap requirement and Phase 17 verification claim.
