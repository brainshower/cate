## Evidence Notes

- Final verification must replace every `PENDING` manual field above with concrete `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `BLOCKED` evidence.
- Screenshot files, Playwright/Electron run notes, or source-location notes are acceptable evidence when they identify the exact inspected surface.
- Failures or blockers must remain explicit; do not convert a blocked visual check into a pass without running-app evidence.
